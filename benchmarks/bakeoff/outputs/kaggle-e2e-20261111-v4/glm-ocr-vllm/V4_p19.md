## POTATO

## (Solanum tuberosum)

[ ]

Variety: 'Wilwash' Application No. 91/044

Application received: 17 April 1991

Applicant: Daratech Pty Ltd, of Melbourne, Victoria.

'Wilwash' is a smooth-skinned round potato with white flesh and white skin. It has few flowers with white corolla and the lightsprout base anthocyanin is blue-violet. It is distinct from known varieties in having the following combination of characters: leaflets absent or few in the axils of the inflorescence, short flowering period, earlier plant maturity.

## Diagnosis

## Varieties used for comparison

'Coliban' being the closest known variety, an industry standard variety and one of the parent varieties.

## Comparative Growing Trials

All characteristics described below are from comparative growing trials conducted at the Potato Research Station, Toolangi in Victoria in 1989/90 and 1990/91. Two hundred plants were grown in three replicates on kraznozem soil fertilised with Pivot 800 at 2t/ha. Measurements are from 20 specimens selected at random. Plants were irrigated as required and prophylactic sprays were applied four times for fungal diseases of foliage.

## Origin

This variety arose from the controlled pollination of the variety 'Norgleam' by the pollen parent 'Coliban'. It was bred by Dr Roger Kirkham of Healesville, Victoria between 1980 and 1991. 'Wilwash' was selected for its superior yield, smooth white-skinned tubers of uniform size, shape and cooking qualities from 20,000 field grown plants.
