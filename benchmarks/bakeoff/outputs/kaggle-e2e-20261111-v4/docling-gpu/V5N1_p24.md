## Comparative Growing Trials

All characteristics described are from comparative growing trials conducted at Narromine, New South Wales in autumn 1990.

Varieties were seeded in February and transplanted into the field in March 1990. Plants were spaced 36 cm within and 50 cm between rows on 75 cm beds. Kerb herbicide was applied   subsequent to transplanting and Ridomil was applied twice during the latter half of the growing period.
