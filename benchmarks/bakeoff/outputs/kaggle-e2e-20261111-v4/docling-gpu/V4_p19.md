## POTATO

## (Solanum tuberosum)

<!-- image -->

Variety: 'Wilwash' Application No. 91/044 Application received: 17 April 1991 Applicant: Daratech Ltd, of Melbourne, Victoria. Pty

## Diagnosis

Wilwash is a smooth-skinned round potato with white flesh and white skin. It has few flowers with white corolla and the lightsprout base anthocyanin is blue-violet: It is distinct from known varieties in having the following combination of characters: leaflets absent or few in the axils of the inflorescence, short flowering period, earlier plant maturity:

## Varieties used for comparison

'Coliban' being the closest known variety, an industry standard variely and one of the parent varieties.

## Comparative Growing Trials

All characteristics described below are from comparative growing trials conducted at the Potato Research Station, Toolangi in Victoria in 1989/90 and 1990/91\_ Two hundred plants were grown in three replicates on kraznozem soil fertilised with Pivot 800 at 2t/ha Measurements are from 20 specimens selected at random. Plants were irrigated as required and prophylactic sprays were applied four limes for fungal diseases of foliage.
