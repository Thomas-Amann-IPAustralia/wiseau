Agronomy
'Bluey' is intended for cropping in low to medium rainfall
(300-500 mm p.a.) regions.

OBJECTIONS

Formal objections (S20, PVR Act 1987): against any of the
above applications can be lodged with the Registrar by a
person who:

a) considers their commercial interests would be
affected by a grant of PVR to the applicant; AND
b)considers that the provisions of S26 cannot be
met.

A fee of $200 is payable at the time of lodging a formal
objection. An additional $70/hour will be charged if the
examination of the objection by the PVR Office takes more
than two hours.

Comment: Any person may make written comment at no
charge on the eligibility of the above applications for PVR.

A person submitting a formal objection or a comment must
provide supporting evidence to substantiate the claim. A
copy of the submission will also be sent to the applicant
and the latter will be asked to show why the objection
should not be upheld.

All formal objections and comments relating to the above
applications must be lodged with the Registrar by close of
business on 30 September, 1991.
