## Morphology — see comparison tables.

'Cecilia' is a low bi-coloured cluster-flowered rose with flowers larger than those of 'Cream Puff' and similar in size to 'Summer Breeze'. The undulating thick petals give the flower a cupped appearance similar to 'Summer Breeze' but unlike the flat shape of 'Cream Puff'. The appearance of the flower can be compared with the larger shrub roses 'Fruhlingsmorgen' and 'Sparrieshoop'. The foliage is matt and of a lighter green than that of the comparative varieties. The stipules of 'Cecilia' are significantly shorter than those of the comparative varieties.

## Diagnosis

'Coco' (Korferse) is a medium red glasshouse rose. It is distinct from other known varieties in having the following combination of characters: whole bloom closest to RHS 44B; petal basal spot present only on the inside.
