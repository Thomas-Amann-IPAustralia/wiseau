Variety: ‘Mechta’ Application No. 88/020

Diagnosis
This variety is distinct from any other known
variety in having the following combination of
characteristics:

a large diameter double flower with many petals,
some arranged in a thick central cluster; a dark
violet petal colour corresponding to RHS 74A; petal
margins with large even cserrations; an epicalyx
with 3 pairs of opposite lobes; along thick stem;
large foliage (very wide leaves) and calyx with a
thick wax coating; and a calyx resistant to splitting.
