10.

A bacterial isolate, Kurthia sp, (MTCC 5181) deposited at International Depository at
IMTECH, Sector 39A, Chandigarh, India recognized by Budapest Treaty.
An isolate as claimed in claim 1 wherein the isolate is capable of growth in a medium of
pH range of 10.00 -12.00.
An isolate as claimed in claim 1 wherein the bacterial isolate is capable of lowering pH
(12.0 to 11.5) of textile industrial wastewater to neutral pH (7.5 to 7.00) within a short
period of about two hours.
An isolate as claimed in claim 1 wherein the bacterial isolate is capable of neutralizing
high pH (12.0 to 11.5) of textile industrial wastewater to neutral pH (7.5 to 7.0) when
used in a ratio ranging from 1:5 to 1:10.
An isolate as claimed in claim 1 wherein the bacterial isolate is isolated from soil
accumulated in a pipe of an effluent treatment plant of textile industry located in
Chandigarh, India, through which alkaline wastewater has passed.
An isolate as claimed in claim 1 wherein the bacterial isolate Kurthia sp is Gram -
Positive, motile, catalase positive, and capable of reducing nitrate and hydrolyzing
starch.
A process of preparing a bacterial isolate of bacterium Kurthia sp, (MTCC 5181) useful
in neutralization of alkaline waste water (pH-12.00 to 11.5) of textile industry and
deposited at International Depository at IMTECH, Sector 39A, Chandigarh, India
recognized by Budapest Treaty, the process comprising:
(a) enriching soil contaminated with bacteria by adding a soil extract in basal carbonate
medium,
(b) culturing the bacteria using the basal carbonate medium;
(c) isolating the bacteria from the culture obtained from step (b) to obtain a pellet of
bacterial cells;
(d) dissolving the pellet obtained from step (c) in phosphate buffer.
A process as claimed in claim 7 wherein step (a) is carried out for a period of 72-96
hours at 100-120 rpm and at a temperature in the range of 35-3 TC.
A process as claimed in claim 7 wherein the ratio of soil extract to basal carbonate
medium is in the range of 1:1-1:2.
A process as claimed in claim 7 wherein the bacteria is cultured in step (b) at a pH in

the range of 1.5-12.00 and at a temperature in the range of 35-3 7°C for 8-10 hours.
